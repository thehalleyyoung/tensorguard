## Decidability / performance trade-offs

- **QF_LIA + finite domains.** shape (matmul/linear/cat/broadcast), device, phase -- decidable in P (Tinelli-Zarba combination of QF_LIA with the finite device/phase theories)
- **NIA (reshape/flatten).** NP-hard (SUBSET-PRODUCT reduction) but still decidable; both solvers must agree on these too
- **Performance (qualitative, machine-dependent, not byte-diffed).** On these small VCs both solvers answer in well under a millisecond; Z3 is the default because of its UserPropagator interface (used by the DPLL(T) shape propagators), while cvc5 provides an independent cross-check that the verdicts are not a Z3 idiosyncrasy.

> Any VC where Z3 and cvc5 disagree, or either is wrong, is a soundness trust-boundary failure. The verifier's soundness must not depend on the choice of SMT backend.
