## Theorem (validated)

**Tight CEGAR Convergence via Predicate Coverage.** The CEGAR loop terminates in at most k = |P_final \ P_seed| iterations, where k ≤ (1 - coverage) × |P_prog|.

_When coverage ≈ 1 (guards capture most predicates), k ≈ 0 and CEGAR converges in O(1) iterations. The adversarial case (coverage ≈ 0) recovers the naive bound |P_prog|._
