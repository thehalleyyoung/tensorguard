Abstract
T ENSOR G UARD is a no-execution refinement-type checker for PyTorch
nn.Module forward methods that verifies tensor shapes and gradient flow statically
from class source, without instantiating the module or tracing it. On a curated
60-bug historical corpus it returns R EFUTED -P ROOF on 53/60 (88.3%, Wilson
95% CI [77.8%, 94.2%]); on the N =34 fragment-fair head-to-head with Pytea,
32/34 vs. 22/34 (McNemar exact p=0.00195); and on a pre-registered post-freeze
real-PR sample drawn with no selection for fragment fit, 5/15 catches versus
FakeTensorMode 2/15 and Pytea 3/15 (a directional result on N =15, not statistically separable from either baseline at α=0.05 and reported as such). The
contribution is a refinement-type calculus and an assume/guarantee discipline at
the class boundary; the shape-transfer rule table is mechanised in Lean 4 (28 of
79 handlers with 11/11 soundness lemmas closed sorry-free), while the analyser
implementation, AST extractor, backward verifier, and Z3 dispatch remain in the
trusted computing base. On the 488-block real-source corpus the user-visible freesymbolic regime produces 0 unconditional R EFUTED -P ROOF verdicts; that surface
is reported as a fragment-coverage measurement, not as a bug-finding result. An
open-source implementation accompanies the paper.

Introduction

Tensor programs in PyTorch fail in two characteristic ways that unit tests rarely catch: (i) the forward
pass crashes deep inside an autograd-traced subgraph because two operand shapes disagree under a
particular batch size, sequence length, or image resolution; and (ii) the backward pass silently produces a wrong gradient — e.g. a parameter receives p.grad=None after loss.backward() because
an in-place op or a .detach() broke the autograd tape along one branch, and optimizer.step()
silently skips it.
The PyTorch ecosystem responds to these failures with execution-based tools: torch.fx+ShapeProp
captures one trace, FakeTensorMode executes shape arithmetic on metadata-only tensors,
torch.export specialises a trace, and TorchDynamo installs runtime guards that recompile when an
invariant fails. All four require the model to be importable, instantiable, and traceable on the target machine. On modern HuggingFace/timm code that is typically not the case: a transformer block needs a
config object, an attention head reads self.config.hidden size, and an nn.ModuleList over
config.num layers blocks demands a fully-realised configuration before a single tensor can flow.
T ENSOR G UARD takes the opposite stance. Given an nn.Module class source, with no instantiation
and no tracer, it computes a refinement-typed signature Tensor{s, g | φ} for every value flowing
through forward, where s is a symbolic shape, g is a static gradient-required flag, and φ is a Z3Submitted to 38th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2024). Do not distribute.

Out-of-fragment. The following constructs are not in FTG : data-dependent control flow (condition
involves a runtime tensor value); custom autograd.Function; dynamic attribute mutation of a

Theorem 1 (Fragment-level soundness). Let M ∈ FTG be a closed module and let Γ ⊢ M : τ
with Verify(M ) = V ERIFIED. Then for every input x with σ |= assume M (x), the reduction
⟨M (x), σ⟩ →∗ v does not invoke the operator-level shape-mismatch exception of any operator in
Cat.
Sketch. Preservation: by induction on the BNF of FTG . Each operator rule (T-M ATMUL, TV IEW (−1), T-D ETACH, and the 76 remaining rules in Section B) produces a uniquely-typed result;
the 28 Lean-audited handlers (Table 7) are sorry-free at the rule definitions; the 7 pen-and-paper
handlers follow from the operator-level argument in Section C; the 44 tested-only handlers are not
covered by the soundness theorem. Progress: each statement in FTG either reduces or is a value;
the shape-determinism restriction on if† and for† ensures branch selection and loop unrolling are
determined by the static refinements, so no stuck state arises at those forms. Together, no well-typed
term in the fragment raises a shape-mismatch at any op ∈ Cat; the claim is restricted to modeled
operators.
Boundary precision. Theorem 1 does not claim: (a) termination; (b) numerical correctness
or absence of NaN/Inf; (c) absence of dtype errors outside Cat; (d) soundness of the analyser

Theorem 2 (Soundness of the proof-grade verdicts). For any closed e in the supported fragment
and any heap σ |= Γ: (i) if Verify(e) = V ERIFIED, no reduction sequence from ⟨e, σ, on⟩ raises a

permute, relu, cat, sum reduce, mean reduce, expand, gather, embedding, conv2d, einsum,
unbind, and reshape; 15 of the 17 operators carry a per-operator applyOpExt sound * lemma

witness. The mechanised fragment does not cover the remaining 62 handlers of Table 7, the AST

Extended fragment and front-end

T ENSOR G UARD’s analyser parses class source via Python’s ast module, extracts a typed IR, and
dispatches each op to a handler that returns the refinement-typed result type. We add four front-end
families under (no edits to legacy ). The symbolic config environment binds self.config.<attr>
to fresh SymInts so that HuggingFace-style modules whose dimensions come from a config
object can be checked once and reused. The QKV unpacking front-end recognises the four idiomatic Q/K/V splits (split, chunk, view+unbind, and einops.rearrange with the canonical
(three h d) → three b h t d pattern) and elaborates each into three correlated tensor types.
The negative-one reshape elaborator emits a Z3 divisibility constraint P mod Q = 0. Transfer
rules for F.scaled dot product attention, nn.MultiheadAttention, nn.LayerNorm, and
nn.RMSNorm register into the analyser’s shape-op dictionary. All 74 unit tests pass on python3.11
+ torch 2.9.1. We report that on the legacy v4 corpus these new APIs produce zero verdict-count
delta (the v4 corpus did not abstain on patterns these APIs target); the v5 contribution is that the
new APIs are now callable on realistic Transformer modules that v4 could not enter, surfaced in the
empirical evaluation through the 488-block real-source corpus (Section 4.1).

The backward verifier consumes a forward graph annotated with the refinement types of Section 2 and
certifies, without execution, that (i) loss.backward() would produce gradients of the right shape
modulo sum to(grad,primal.shape) reduction, and (ii) the set H of parameters reverse-reachable
from loss along non-detached, non-no grad-context, requires grad=True edges equals the userdeclared expected-to-learn set E. The check is a reverse-topological traversal mirroring PyTorch’s
per-Function shape contract grad_outputs[i].shape == y_i.shape. The grad-flag component
additionally detects the three canonical silent-zero-grad bug classes: in-place writes on a leaf with
requires grad=True; no leaf in the trace has requires grad=True; and parameters used only
inside torch.no grad() or after .detach(). These are reported as R EFUTED with witness traces.

Localisation tracer and hybrid mode

Localisation. A v4 review noted that of 6 detections in early experiments, only 1 landed within ±5
lines of the bug. The v4 verifier returns location.line=0 for bugs detected via the model-check
path (those that bypass per-step counterexamples). We add a “first unsatisfied symbolic constraint”
tracer : given a refutation, we extract the offending tensor and dim variables from the bug message
and walk the source AST to the earliest assignment or typing-rule call that bound a symbolic dim
appearing in the unsatisfied constraint, attributing the bug to its source line.

208

Hybrid mode (TG-static → FakeTensor fallback). tensorguard.hybrid check(M) returns
the TG verdict if it is V ERIFIED or R EFUTED; otherwise, if M is importable and instantiable with
a no-arg constructor, it falls back to FakeTensorMode. Both the localisation tracer and the hybrid
mode are evaluated in Section 4.2.

N/A (tool structurally inapplicable). TG’s R EFUTED column is further split into the three sub-verdicts
of Theorem 2: R EFUTED -P ROOF (RP, an unconditional witness), C ONTRACT-V IOLATION (CV,
sound under the caller-respects-precondition assumption), and L IBRARY-WARN (LW, conservative
warning on dispatch outside the modeled fragment). Theorem 2 covers RP and CV; LW is reported
but not a soundness claim. We never silently report V ERIFIED when an analyser was unable to reason:
each A BSTAIN is tagged with one reason from a fixed top-10 taxonomy.
Headline. Calibration first. On the 488-block real-source corpus, TG produces zero unconditional
R EFUTED -P ROOF verdicts. The 206 refutations decompose as 128 C ONTRACT-V IOLATION (sound
only under the synthesised caller-rely assume) plus 78 L IBRARY-WARN (conservative warnings
outside the soundness theorem). The 57 V ERIFIED verdicts also rest in part on synthesised config
envelopes; under a regime where every self.config.X is treated as a free symbolic variable with
no upper-bound assumption, the user-visible verdict triple becomes 34 V / 0 RP / 206 LW / 248 A

unconditional-RP claim is carried by the bug corpora, not by the block corpus.
Caller-rely satisfiability of the 128 CV verdicts. A C ONTRACT-V IOLATION is meaningful only if
its synthesised caller-rely assume M is satisfied by at least one real caller. We mechanically classify
all 128 CV-verdict modules on the 488-block corpus by what their assume M references: 26/128
have an empty assume M (trivially satisfied), 90/128 reduce to symbolic references over documented
config attributes (config.hidden size, config.num attention heads, . . . ) that every published checkpoint config exposes, and 12/128 are *PreTrainedModel stubs whose init is
inherited and contributes no axiom. Zero assume M is unwitnessed: every CV refutes against at least
one realisable caller pattern, never the empty contract. We additionally exhibit the joint-realisability
check on the full 128 CV set: each row’s full assume M conjunction is evaluated against a single default *Config() instantiation of its natural caller. The witnessed-ratio is 118/128 (92.2%,
Clopper-Pearson 95% CI [86.1%, 96.2%]); the 10 non-witnessed rows are all *PreTrainedModel
stubs or aliasing-only constructors whose default *Config() omits one declared sym-attr, never a

GPT-2 (2), BERT/Llama/Mistral/Falcon/Qwen2/RoBERTa/Albert/Bloom (1 each); zero are torchvision or timm. All 10 live in the same bucket (symbolic-config-only) with the identical diagnostic “one

Dynamo-guard correspondence

Theorem 5 (Dynamo-guard correspondence; necessary direction). Let M be an nn.Module in the
supported fragment with T ENSOR G UARD contract C(M ) = (A, G). Then, on the intersection of the
TG fragment and Dynamo’s traceable subset, TG-abstention is necessary for guard-stability: if TG
reports V ERIFIED on M then every shape/dtype/rank refinement bit Dynamo reads on the trace at
any input x with A(x) true is a refinement variable in φ for some rule in the Section 2 catalogue. The
converse is not promised in either direction: Dynamo additionally specialises on metadata outside the
TG fragment (e.g. Python ints, list lengths, integer parameters captured at trace time), and therefore
may install guards (and recompile) on inputs that satisfy C(M ). Outside the catalogue intersection,
TG abstains and Dynamo breaks the graph.
Sketch. Each typing rule’s premise is a function of fake-tensor metadata bits Dynamo’s specialiser

reads element count and explicit factors, etc.); details in Section F. The reverse direction fails by
inspection: Dynamo’s guard set strictly extends the shape contract.

T ENSOR G UARD sits at the intersection of refinement types, abstract interpretation for ML, and the
PyTorch shape ecosystem.
Refinement and dependent types. Liquid Haskell [Vazou et al., 2014a], F⋆ [Swamy et al., 2016],
Dafny [Leino, 2010], Lean [de Moura and Ullrich, 2021], Dependent Haskell [Eisenberg, 2016], and
singletons [Eisenberg and Weirich, 2012] establish the SMT-decidable / dependent-typing line;
Hasktorch applied type-level naturals to deep learning. We take Liquid Haskell’s SMT-decidable design, specialise the refinement language to PyTorch tensor metadata, and add the grad-flag dimension.
Abstract interpretation and shape verifiers for ML. Ariadne [Dolby et al., 2018] pioneered abstract shape inference for TensorFlow; DeepPoly [Singh et al., 2019] and AI2 [Gehr et al., 2018] certify
robustness on values assuming shapes are correct; jaxtyping/torchtyping are runtime checkers.
TG differs by symbolic, ahead-of-tracer shape reasoning that additionally models requires grad.

Limitations and Conclusion

Scope. We analyse the typed AST fragment FTG (Section 2.1): nn.Module classes with statically
resolvable layer constructors, straight-line forward (with shape-deterministic if† /for† ), and the
operator catalogue of Section A. Decorator metaprogramming, runtime attribute mutation, custom
autograd.Function subclasses, and truly data-dependent control flow are out-of-scope and trigger
sound A BSTAIN — the same intersection on which the necessary direction of Theorem 5 holds. The
backward verifier tracks grad refinements and severed-tape conditions; it does not verify numerical
correctness or second-order derivatives. The soundness theorem (Theorem 2) covers the RP and CV
verdicts only; LW is a conservative warning. Lean does not mechanise the AST extractor, the analyser
implementation, or the backward verifier; the assume/guarantee composition rule is mechanised on
the 17-operator ag composition ext fragment, and the boundary for the remaining 62 handlers is
shown in Table 7.
Semantically-aliased view bugs. A residual class of real upstream bugs is undetectable by any
purely shape-arithmetic check: the buggy and the (upstream-fix) correct view targets agree on total element count for the input shape that exercises the bug, and the symptom is a silently-wrong
tensor permutation rather than a runtime RuntimeError. On the upstream-faithful re-extraction
of the 10-bug real-public corpus (Table 2, 2/10 silent verifieds on rb 001, rb 002) this is the
precise residual failure mode: the input shape (2, 4, 2·64, 192) has the same product as both
view(2,4,2,64,192) (buggy) and any equivalent 5-dim factoring whose trailing dim divides
192. On these two inputs the synthesised assume M is concretely populated: the AST extractor
binds {num heads=4, num chunks=2, chunk size=64, dqk=192} from the upstream init
defaults , Theorem 2 is satisfied non-vacuously, and the analyser correctly returns “no shape arithmetic
violation” — the silent-verified verdict is therefore consistent with Theorem 2 and not a vacuousassumption artefact. Catching this class requires either an upstream operator-intent specification (e.g.
a typed einops target) or an input-distribution-driven semantic check; both are out of scope for the
static, no-execution refinement-type fragment we analyse. The earlier limitation (”constructor-bound
integer attributes are not yet propagated into the envelope synthesiser”) has been closed in the implementation: the per-forward local-scalar map and the nn.Parameter alias step convert rb 004 and
rb 010 from silent verifieds into RP@0.99; the init-time local-scalar fold, single-dim shape-alias
recogniser, and shape-tuple propagator (with starred view(*new shape) expansion) additionally
convert rb 003 and rb 006, lifting the upstream-faithful corpus to 7/10 unconditional RP.
Constructor-bound integer-attribute envelope (residual). A second residual class is ctor-bound
integer arithmetic where the buggy comparison is between two statically-known integers but the
result of the comparison is not folded by the static-integer subset of the TG fragment. Examples
on the post-freeze hold-out (Table 3, rb pf 002, rb pf 005, rb pf 006) have the TG fragment’s
static integer subset populated (assume M binds the relevant constructor scalars to concrete ints;
see ), but the buggy edge appears at a per-call shape comparison TG’s envelope synthesiser does
not currently fire on (e.g. a runtime broadcast-add between (B, N, S, S) and (B, 1, S, S+1)
where S is fully concrete on both sides; the static arithmetic shows S̸=S+1, but the broadcast18

refinement-typed shape-and-grad calculus with assume/guarantee composition at the class boundary,

The decisive findings are: an inapplicability gap on 481/488 real nn.Modules for execution-based
baselines; 53/60 R EFUTED -P ROOF on a historical corpus and, on upstream-faithful re-extracts of

11/11 Lean soundness lemmas closed sorry-free; and a 25-block hybrid stress set on which TG and
FakeTensor are complementary, not coincident.

Full proof of Theorem 2. Let the small-step relation be ⟨e, σ, G⟩ → ⟨e′ , σ ′ , G′ ⟩ where σ is a heap of
(shape, dtype, requires grad, is on tape) records and G is the ambient grad context. We write
σ |= Γ when every binding x : T in Γ has σ(x) satisfy the shape predicate of T and the runtime
(requires grad ∧ is on tape) bit implies φgrad in the lattice.
Preservation up to subtyping. If Γ ⊢ e : T and σ |= Γ and the step is taken, there exist Γ′ ⊇ Γ
and T ′ with σ ′ |= Γ′ , Γ′ ⊢ e′ : T ′ , and T ′ ⊑ T . By induction on the reduction relation. For each
non-context rule, the operator’s transfer function in produces, by construction, the unique well-formed
shape of the result up to SMT equivalence; preservation on shapes is therefore equality modulo Γ′ .
For grad, T-N O G RAD C TX discharges that the body of torch.no grad() produces a tensor with
is on tape = false, so no grad is sound; T-D ETACH likewise. For differentiable ops with G = on,
PyTorch sets is on tape = true on the result iff at least one input has it true, which is exactly the
join g. Context rules use the IH together with monotonicity of g on the lattice. Subtyping is needed
only at T-N O G RAD C TX exit and at conditional joins where ⊤ is introduced.
Three-valued progress (proof-grade). V ERIFIED means every operator’s shape precondition is entailed
by Γ via Z3; preservation guarantees the precondition holds at the moment of evaluation, so no
shape-mismatch reduction can occur. R EFUTED -P ROOF means some precondition’s negation is
entailed by Γ on every model satisfying Γ, and the witness is reachable under the empty caller-rely;

Lean residual: permList compose

1157

The previously-residual sorry concerned the rule permList compose, originally stated as
σ2 (σ1 (L)) = (σ2 ◦ σ1 )(L) for total permutations L, σi . Lean’s List.get?-with-default-0 semantics
breaks the identity at out-of-range indices — a counterexample is L=[a], σ1 =[1, 0] (out-of-range

∀i ∈ [0, |L|), σ2 (σ1 )(i) < |L|) and is closed sorry-free by pointwise extensionality on the List.map
form. permList is not in the TG forward-rule path that Theorem 2 or the audit JSON depends on;
