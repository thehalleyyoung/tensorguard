1. @thehalleyyoung

As the project grows, additional maintainers are appended to the rotation order
above and to the table. The lead rotates to the next active maintainer in the
list, wrapping around to the top.

### Handover checklist

At each rotation the outgoing lead completes the checklist in
`GOVERNANCE.md#maintainer-rotation` and updates the **Current lead** line above
with the new holder and the effective date.

## Becoming a maintainer

Open a sustained track record of high-quality, soundness-respecting
contributions, then ask an existing maintainer to nominate you. Confirmation is
by lazy consensus of the current maintainers (`GOVERNANCE.md`).
