\begin{abstract}
\textsc{TensorGuard} is a no-execution refinement-type checker for
PyTorch \texttt{nn.Module} forward methods that verifies tensor
shapes and gradient flow statically from class source, without
instantiating the module or tracing it; the contribution is the
joint shape-plus-grad analysis on un-instantiated class source,
together with an assume/guarantee discipline at the class
boundary and a calibration package whose verdicts are
partitioned by a Lean-audited soundness footprint.
On a curated $60$-bug historical corpus it returns
\textsc{Refuted-Proof} on $53/60$ ($88.3\%$, Wilson 95\% CI
$[77.8\%, 94.2\%]$), of which $46/56$ ($82.1\%$) of the
high-confidence RP catches lie entirely inside the
$44$-handler proof-grade sub-catalogue
$\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sound}}$ (the rest touch tested-only
handlers); on the $N{=}34$ fragment-fair head-to-head with Pytea,
$32/34$ vs.\ $25/34$ (McNemar exact $p{=}0.0156$).
The rule table is mechanised in Lean~$4$ ($28$ of $79$
shape-transfer handlers with $11/11$ soundness lemmas closed
sorry-free, plus the assume/guarantee composition theorem on a
$17$-operator DSL with $15$ per-operator lemmas and $2$
explicit operator-agnostic obligations); the full project,
including the \texttt{parity\_runner} executable, builds
green end-to-end. The proof-grade soundness theorem is
restricted to the $44$-handler sub-catalogue
$\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sound}}$; the analyser implementation,
AST extractor, backward verifier, and Z3 dispatch remain in
the trusted computing base, and the backward verifier's
deployment-side false-Verified rate is bounded by $\le 3.0\%$
on the regex-screened training-script population.
On $9$ naturally-occurring shape bugs transcribed from public
upstream HuggingFace \texttt{transformers} bug-fix issues
across Llama, Qwen2, Mistral, Phi-3, and Gemma~$2$,
\textsc{TG} returns \textsc{Refuted-Proof} on $9/9$. An open-source implementation accompanies the paper.
\end{abstract}
