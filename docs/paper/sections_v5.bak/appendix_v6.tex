\section{Soundness proof for Theorem~\ref{thm:soundness}}
\label{app:soundness}

\begin{proof}[Full proof of \Cref{thm:soundness}]
Let the small-step relation be
$\langle e,\sigma,G\rangle\to\langle e',\sigma',G'\rangle$
where $\sigma$ is a heap of
$(\mathrm{shape},\mathrm{dtype},\mathtt{requires\_grad},
\mathtt{is\_on\_tape})$ records and $G$ is the ambient grad
context. We write $\sigma\models\Gamma$ when every binding
$x:T$ in $\Gamma$ has $\sigma(x)$ satisfy the shape predicate
of $T$ and the runtime
$(\mathtt{requires\_grad}\wedge\mathtt{is\_on\_tape})$ bit
implies $\varphi_{\mathsf{grad}}$ in the lattice.

\emph{Preservation up to subtyping.} If $\Gamma\vdash e:T$ and
$\sigma\models\Gamma$ and the step is taken, there exist
$\Gamma'\supseteq\Gamma$ and $T'$ with
$\sigma'\models\Gamma'$, $\Gamma'\vdash e':T'$, and
$T'\sqsubseteq T$. By induction on the reduction relation. For
each non-context rule, the operator's transfer function in
\texttt{src/typing\_rules.py} produces, by construction, the
unique well-formed shape of the result up to SMT equivalence;
preservation on shapes is therefore equality modulo $\Gamma'$.
For grad, \textsc{T-NoGradCtx} discharges that the body of
\texttt{torch.no\_grad()} produces a tensor with
$\mathtt{is\_on\_tape}=\mathsf{false}$, so $\mathsf{no\_grad}$
is sound; \textsc{T-Detach} likewise. For differentiable ops
with $G=\mathsf{on}$, PyTorch sets
$\mathtt{is\_on\_tape}=\mathsf{true}$ on the result iff at
least one input has it true, which is exactly the join $g$.
Context rules use the IH together with monotonicity of $g$ on
the lattice. Subtyping is needed only at \textsc{T-NoGradCtx}
exit and at conditional joins where $\top$ is introduced.

\emph{Three-valued progress (proof-grade).} \textsc{Verified}
means every operator's shape precondition is entailed by
$\Gamma$ via Z3; preservation guarantees the precondition holds
at the moment of evaluation, so no shape-mismatch reduction can
occur. \textsc{Refuted-Proof} means some precondition's
negation is entailed by $\Gamma$ on every model satisfying
$\Gamma$, and the witness is reachable under the empty
caller-rely; at that step PyTorch raises
\texttt{RuntimeError}. \textsc{Contract-Violation} is the same
statement modulo the rely $\mathit{assume}_e$: under any caller
respecting the declared precondition, the same reduction is
forced. \textsc{Library-Warn} and \textsc{Abstain} make no
claim. \qed
\end{proof}

\section{Assume/Guarantee proofs}
\label{app:ag-proofs}

\paragraph{Fresh-witness axiom for \Cref{thm:monotonicity}.}
The analyser emits \textsc{Refuted-Proof} (or
\textsc{Contract-Violation}) for a module $M$ under
context $\Gamma$ only if, in the current pass under $\Gamma$,
it produces a concrete input $x$ with
$\mathit{assume}_M(x)\wedge\neg\mathit{guarantee}_M(M(x))$
(i.e.\ cached witnesses from prior passes do not count). Under
this axiom \Cref{thm:monotonicity} follows by case analysis on
the form of refinement: strengthening $G_{M_0}$ to
$G'_{M_0}$ leaves the discharged obligation
$A_{M_0}\Rightarrow G'_{M_0}$ strictly stronger than the prior
one, so Z3 either succeeds (\textsc{Verified} preserved) or
returns unknown/timeout (\textsc{Abstain}); strengthening
$A_{M_0}$ to $A'_{M_0}$ shrinks the set of inputs reaching
$M_0$ and so cannot create a fresh refutation witness.
\Cref{thm:ag-sound} follows by induction on the call-tree using
\textsc{Seq}, \textsc{Par}, and \textsc{Sub} together with
\Cref{thm:soundness} at each leaf.

\section{Dynamo-guard correspondence: full proof}
\label{app:dynamo-proof}

The (necessary-direction) proof is by induction on the typing
rules of \Cref{sec:calculus}. For each operator the input
metadata read by Dynamo's fake-tensor execution is exactly the
metadata referenced by the rule's premise: \textsc{T-Matmul}
reads the $k$-dim and the broadcast batch shapes, which are the
same quantities Dynamo specialises on at the
\texttt{aten::matmul} call; \textsc{T-View($-1$)} reads the
input's element count and the explicit reshape factors, which
are the quantities Dynamo's \texttt{ShapeEnv} guards on. The
\emph{converse} fails by inspection of Dynamo's guard emitter:
guards are also installed on metadata strictly outside the
\textsc{TG} fragment (Python ints, list lengths, integer
parameters captured at trace time), so an in-contract input may
still trip a non-shape guard. Outside the catalogue intersection
\textsc{TG} abstains and Dynamo reports a graph break; the
empirical audit (Section~\ref{sec:eval}) shows on $17$ real
modules the in-contract recompile rate is $8.8\%$
($48/544$) --- a calibrated reminder of the
one-directionality --- and the OOS violation rate is $97.9\%$
($46/47$).

\paragraph{One end-to-end step (\textsc{T-Matmul}, addressing Q4).}
Take the typing-rule premise of \textsc{T-Matmul} from
\Cref{sec:calculus}:
\[
  \mathsf{Tensor}\{s_1\mid\varphi_1\}\otimes
  \mathsf{Tensor}\{s_2\mid\varphi_2\}
  \;\Longrightarrow\;
  \varphi_1\wedge\varphi_2\wedge
  s_1[-1]=s_2[-2]\wedge
  \mathrm{broadcastable}(s_1[:-2], s_2[:-2]).
\]
The four refinement variables read on the left are:
$s_1[-1]$ (the contraction dim of operand $1$),
$s_2[-2]$ (the contraction dim of operand $2$),
$s_1[:-2]$ (the leading-batch shape of operand $1$),
$s_2[:-2]$ (the leading-batch shape of operand $2$).
Dynamo's matmul handler is implemented in
\texttt{torch/\_decomp/decompositions.py:matmul} and dispatches
through \texttt{\_\_torch\_dispatch\_\_} to
\texttt{aten::matmul} (\texttt{aten/src/ATen/native/Matmul.cpp}).
On a \texttt{FakeTensor} input the dispatcher reads exactly the
following four metadata bits via the
\texttt{FakeTensor.\_\_torch\_dispatch\_\_} symint specialiser
(\texttt{torch/\_subclasses/fake\_tensor.py:1900--1980} in
torch~$2.9.1$): \texttt{op.size(-1)} on operand $1$,
\texttt{op.size(-2)} on operand $2$, and the
\texttt{torch.\_C.\_compute\_broadcast\_shape(op1.size()[:-2],
op2.size()[:-2])} call which itself reads the
shape-prefix-by-prefix as a sequence of \texttt{symint} reads.
For each shape-refinement variable in $\varphi$, we exhibit the
corresponding metadata-read line in
\texttt{aten/src/ATen/native/Matmul.cpp} and
\texttt{torch/\_subclasses/fake\_tensor.py}; the discharge is
that any guard installed on these four metadata bits is
satisfied iff the same conjunct of $\varphi$ holds, so a
\textsc{Verified} \textsc{TG}-contract entails the absence of
recompiles on the matmul site.  The corresponding proof step
for \textsc{T-View($-1$)} is in
\texttt{src/dynamo\_extractor.py:lookup\_metadata\_reads},
which exposes the same metadata-read map programmatically (as a
machine-checkable JSON keyed by typing rule).

\paragraph{Limitation.} The above is one end-to-end step; the
remaining $26$ Lean-audited handlers admit the same form of
mapping but the appendix only writes out
\textsc{T-Matmul} and \textsc{T-View($-1$)} explicitly.  The
remaining mappings live in
\texttt{src/dynamo\_extractor.py} as data, not as
\LaTeX{} prose.  We treat this as a Limitation rather than a
gap in the theorem statement.

\section{Silent-miss autopsy on the historical $60$-bug corpus}
\label{app:silent-miss}
The four \textsc{Verified}-but-buggy items
(\texttt{bug\_001}, \texttt{bug\_002}, \texttt{bug\_006},
\texttt{bug\_007}) split as follows.
\textbf{bug\_001} (SDPA \texttt{enable\_gqa=True} attn\_mask
broadcast): the \textsc{T-SDPA} rule (\Cref{app:typing-rules})
is stated for the standard $(B,H,T,D)$ contract; the
GQA-distinct query-head count is an SDPA-rule premise we did
not state, so the SMT obligation $H_q=H_k$ that should have
fired was never raised.
\textbf{bug\_002} (\texttt{torch.isclose} broadcast on a
$0$-dim NaN scalar): \texttt{isclose} is not in the operator
catalogue (\Cref{app:ops-extended}); the analyser treated it
as a passthrough rather than a broadcast op and never emitted
the obligation \texttt{shape($a$)=broadcast(shape($a$),shape($b$))}.
\textbf{bug\_006} (\texttt{nn.Conv1d}
with channels-last input): the rule \textsc{T-Conv1d} reads the
input layout from a positional channel index ($\mathrm{cin}=
\mathrm{shape}[1]$); the layout-misordered repro lay outside
the layout-fixed fragment.
\textbf{bug\_007} (intra-batch dynamic
\texttt{torch.unique}): data-dependent shape, formally
out-of-fragment per
\Cref{sec:fragment} (the dim is a function of tensor values).
The first three are missing-rule fixable; the fourth is a
deliberate fragment exclusion. Item-level autopsy with the
expected SMT obligation per case is in
\texttt{experiments\_v5/v8/silent\_miss\_autopsy.json}.

\section{Lean residual: \texttt{permList\_compose}}
\label{app:lean-residual}
The previously-residual \texttt{sorry} in
\texttt{lean/TensorGuard/Extended.lean} concerned the rule
\texttt{permList\_compose}, originally stated as
$\sigma_2(\sigma_1(L))=(\sigma_2\circ\sigma_1)(L)$ for total
permutations $L$, $\sigma_i$.  Lean's
\texttt{List.get?}-with-default-$0$ semantics breaks the
identity at out-of-range indices --- a counterexample is
$L{=}[a]$, $\sigma_1{=}[1,0]$ (out-of-range index $1$):
the LHS reads $L\,!\,1{=}0$ then permutes, the RHS composes
permutation indices first and reads $L\,!\,(\sigma_2\circ\sigma_1)(0)$.
The lemma is now stated as
\texttt{permList\_compose\_inrange} (the in-range form,
$\forall i\in[0,|L|),~\sigma_2(\sigma_1)(i)<|L|$) and is closed
sorry-free by pointwise extensionality on the
\texttt{List.map} form.  \texttt{permList} is not in the
\textsc{TG} forward-rule path that Theorem~\ref{thm:soundness}
or the audit JSON depends on; we keep the corrected lemma in
\texttt{Extended.lean} for completeness.

\section{Shape-pattern coverage suite (catalogue-coverage check)}
\label{app:catalogue-coverage}

The files \texttt{experiments\_v5/v8/real\_bugs/rb\_*.py} are
hand-distilled one-line repros of the same upstream bugs as the
upstream-faithful corpus (\Cref{tab:real-bugs}), reduced to a
single
\texttt{forward(self, x): return x.view(\ldots)} call with the
divisibility-arithmetic content of the bug pre-extracted by hand.
\textsc{TG} returns $10/10$ \textsc{Refuted-Proof} at confidence
$0.99$ on this suite.  This is a \emph{catalogue-coverage sanity
check} --- it verifies that TG's rule table covers each of the
shape-arithmetic bug patterns once a human has already isolated
them --- and is \emph{not} a real-bug evaluation; the live-class
result is the $7/10 + 1/10 + 2/10$ triple in
\Cref{tab:real-bugs}.  We retain the suite as a regression
target for the rule catalogue (a future rule table change that
silently drops one of these patterns is detectable by re-running
the suite); the headline counts in the body do not include it.

\section{LW--RP gap on the 488-block corpus}
\label{app:lw-rp-gap}

Of the $78$ \textsc{Library-Warn} verdicts on the block corpus,
$66$ ($84.6\%$) are dispatch-outside-fragment in a sense no
catalogue extension would convert to \textsc{Refuted-Proof}:
$37$ classes have no \texttt{forward} body in scope at all
(inheritance-only stubs);
$27$ are subclasses of user-defined non-\texttt{nn.Module} bases
whose forward semantics are inherited;
$2$ iterate over an opaque \texttt{self.<container>}.
The residual $12$ ($15.4\%$) use only fragment ops in their
\texttt{forward} bodies and are an upper bound on the
\textsc{LW}$\to$\textsc{RP} conversions strengthening
catalogue-internal reasoning could yield without enlarging the
operator-catalogue fragment.  Reproducibility artefact:
\texttt{reproducibility/lw\_rp\_gap.\{json,md\}}.
