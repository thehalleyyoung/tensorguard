\section{Assume/Guarantee Reasoning for \texttt{nn.Module} Composition}
\label{sec:assume-guarantee}

\subsection{Per-module contracts}
\label{sec:ag:contracts}

For each \texttt{nn.Module} $M$ we synthesise a \emph{contract}
\[
 C(M) \;=\; \bigl(\mathit{assume}_M,\ \mathit{guarantee}_M\bigr),
\]
where $\mathit{assume}_M$ is a predicate over input tensor types
(shape refinements with symbolic dimension variables and a Boolean
\texttt{requires\_grad} flag) and $\mathit{guarantee}_M$ is a predicate
over output tensor types in the same language. Both predicates live in
the quantifier-free linear-integer-arithmetic-plus-uninterpreted-functions
fragment discharged by Z3. Contracts are a direct port of Jones's
rely/guarantee discipline for shared-state concurrency~\citep{jones1983tentative}
to the data-flow setting of a deep network: $\mathit{assume}_M$ is the
\emph{rely} on the upstream environment, $\mathit{guarantee}_M$ is what
$M$ promises to its downstream context. The same boundary appears in
Meyer's Design-by-Contract for Eiffel~\citep{meyer1992applying}, and in
the refinement-type contracts of Findler and
Felleisen~\citep{findler2002contracts}, Liquid Haskell~\citep{vazou2014refinement},
and \fstar~\citep{swamy2016dependent}; we inherit their semantics
verbatim, with tensor-shape refinements playing the role of value
refinements and the abstract domain replacing the concrete-value
projection of \citet{cousot1977abstract}.

\subsection{Composition rules}
\label{sec:ag:composition}

\paragraph{Sequential composition.}
For $M_2 \circ M_1$ (modelled by \texttt{nn.Sequential} or by an explicit
forward chain), the proof obligation is the entailment
$\mathit{guarantee}_{M_1}\!\Rightarrow\!\mathit{assume}_{M_2}$,
discharged by Z3. The composite contract is then
$(\mathit{assume}_{M_1},\,\mathit{guarantee}_{M_2})$:
\[
\inferrule[Seq]
{
 \vdash C(M_1)=(A_1,G_1) \quad
 \vdash C(M_2)=(A_2,G_2) \quad
 \models G_1 \Rightarrow A_2
}
{
 \vdash C(M_2 \circ M_1) = (A_1,\,G_2)
}
\]

\paragraph{Parallel / branched composition.}
\texttt{nn.ModuleList}, residual sums, and the QKV split of attention
all share the same shape: a single tensor (or tuple) is fanned out to
$k$ siblings $M_1,\dots,M_k$ whose outputs are recombined by a join
operator $\oplus$ (concatenation, sum, gather). Following the
thread-modular reading of rely/guarantee~\citep{jones1983tentative},
each sibling is verified \emph{independently} under the parent's
guarantee:
\[
\inferrule[Par]
{
 \forall i\in[1,k]:\ \models G_{\text{parent}}\Rightarrow A_{M_i}
 \quad
 \models \bigl(\textstyle\bigwedge_i G_{M_i}\bigr)\Rightarrow A_\oplus
}
{
 \vdash C\bigl(\oplus(M_1,\dots,M_k)\bigr)
 = \bigl(A_{\text{parent}},\ G_\oplus\bigr)
}
\]
The premise structure mirrors the DAG of \texttt{torch.fx} traces and
is what discharges per Dynamo subgraph.

\subsection{Three-valued outcomes and the precision lattice}
\label{sec:ag:three-valued}

The analyser returns one of $\{\textsc{Verified},\textsc{Refuted},\textsc{Abstain}\}$,
interpreted concretisation-style as elements of the powerset
$\mathcal{P}\{\mathsf{ok},\mathsf{bug}\}$:
\[
 \gamma(\textsc{Verified})=\{\mathsf{ok}\},\quad
 \gamma(\textsc{Refuted})=\{\mathsf{bug}\},\quad
 \gamma(\textsc{Abstain})=\{\mathsf{ok},\mathsf{bug}\}.
\]
Ordered by $\subseteq$, this is the precision lattice of
\citet{cousot1977abstract}: \textsc{Verified} and \textsc{Refuted} are
incomparable minimal elements and \textsc{Abstain} is the top
$\top$.\footnote{Hence ``\textsc{Refuted}\!<\!\textsc{Abstain}\!<\!\textsc{Verified}''
is \emph{not} the right order: \textsc{Verified} and \textsc{Refuted}
are equally precise, just disjoint.} This is exactly the gradual
typing reading of \citet{siek2006gradual}: \textsc{Abstain} is the
``unknown'' shape that defers proof to runtime.

We adopt one definitional axiom about how the analyser reports
\textsc{Refuted}, made necessary by the rubber-duck review of
\S\ref{sec:ag:monotonicity}:

\begin{axiom}[Fresh-witness Refutation]
\label{ax:refuted}
The analyser emits \textsc{Refuted} for module $M$ under environment
$\Gamma$ only if, \emph{in the current analysis pass under $\Gamma$},
it produces a concrete input $x$ with
$\mathit{assume}_M(x) \wedge \neg\mathit{guarantee}_M(M(x))$.
Cached witnesses from prior passes do not count.
\end{axiom}

\subsection{Monotonicity of refinement}
\label{sec:ag:monotonicity}

A \emph{contract refinement} $C\sqsubseteq C'$ at module $M_0$ means
$A'_{M_0}\!\Rightarrow\!A_{M_0}$ or $G'_{M_0}\!\Rightarrow\!G_{M_0}$
(strictly smaller assume- or guarantee-sets, in the subtype direction
of \citet{vazou2014refinement} and \citet{swamy2016dependent}); all
other contracts are unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Monotonicity]
\label{thm:monotonicity}
Let $o(\Gamma)\in\{\textsc{Verified},\textsc{Refuted},\textsc{Abstain}\}$
be the analyser's outcome on the root module under contract environment
$\Gamma$. If $o(\Gamma)=\textsc{Verified}$ and $\Gamma'$ refines
$\Gamma$ at any single module $M_0$, then
$o(\Gamma')\in\{\textsc{Verified},\textsc{Abstain}\}$;
in particular $o(\Gamma')\neq\textsc{Refuted}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By cases on the form of refinement.

\emph{(i) $G_{M_0}$ strengthened to $G'_{M_0}$.}
The local obligation becomes $\models A_{M_0}\Rightarrow G'_{M_0}$,
which is strictly stronger than the discharged obligation
$\models A_{M_0}\Rightarrow G_{M_0}$. Z3 either succeeds
(\textsc{Verified} preserved) or returns
\texttt{unknown}/timeout (\textsc{Abstain}). By
Axiom~\ref{ax:refuted}, \textsc{Refuted} requires a \emph{fresh}
concrete witness in the current pass; refining a specification creates
no new concrete inputs, only a new proof obligation. The dual hazard
flagged by the rubber-duck pass---empty $G'_{M_0}$---only weakens
downstream entailments $G'_{M_0}\!\Rightarrow\!A_{\text{child}}$, which
collapse to \textsc{Abstain}, never \textsc{Refuted}.

\emph{(ii) $A_{M_0}$ strengthened to $A'_{M_0}$.}
The local obligation $\forall x.\,A'_{M_0}(x)\!\Rightarrow\!G_{M_0}(M_0(x))$
is \emph{weaker} than the discharged one (smaller domain). The vacuous
sub-case $A'_{M_0}\equiv\bot$ trivially yields \textsc{Verified}. The
new entailment burden $G_{\text{parent}}\!\Rightarrow\!A'_{M_0}$ may
fail, in which case the outcome degrades to \textsc{Abstain}; again,
by Axiom~\ref{ax:refuted}, no new concrete witness is manufactured.

\emph{Composition.} Outcomes combine by greatest-lower-bound on the
lattice of \S\ref{sec:ag:three-valued}, restricted to the set of
\emph{reachable} children (as determined by the \texttt{torch.fx} trace);
unreachable refuted siblings cannot poison the root. Since neither
local case introduces a fresh \textsc{Refuted} leaf along a reachable
path, the root outcome remains in $\{\textsc{Verified},\textsc{Abstain}\}$.
\qed
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:monotonicity} is the formal justification for treating
\textsc{Abstain} as a first-class output rather than engineering laziness:
the analyser is permitted to retreat to $\top$ when contracts are
sharpened, without ever flipping a sound proof into a spurious bug
report.

\subsection{Compositional soundness}
\label{sec:ag:soundness}

\begin{theorem}[Compositional Soundness]
\label{thm:soundness}
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the module tree of an \texttt{nn.Module}. If
(a) every leaf module verifies locally, i.e.\
$\models A_\ell\Rightarrow G_\ell$ for each leaf $\ell$, and
(b) every interface entailment imposed by rules \textsc{Seq} and
\textsc{Par} discharges in Z3, then the root contract is
\textsc{Verified}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By structural induction on $\mathcal{T}$.
\emph{Base:} a leaf is \textsc{Verified} by hypothesis (a).
\emph{Sequential step:} given children $M_1,M_2$ both
\textsc{Verified} with contracts $(A_1,G_1),(A_2,G_2)$, hypothesis
(b) supplies $\models G_1\!\Rightarrow\!A_2$, and rule \textsc{Seq}
yields a \textsc{Verified} composite $(A_1,G_2)$.
\emph{Parallel step:} given siblings $M_1,\dots,M_k$ all
\textsc{Verified} and hypothesis (b) supplying both
$G_{\text{parent}}\!\Rightarrow\!A_{M_i}$ for every $i$ and
$\bigwedge_i G_{M_i}\!\Rightarrow\!A_\oplus$, rule \textsc{Par} yields
a \textsc{Verified} join. Induction concludes at the root.
\qed
\end{proof}

This theorem is the load-bearing whole-tree statement;
the entailment obligations of (b) are exactly the proof goals
enqueued by the compositional-verification pass of the
analyser, and the inter-procedural propagation of contracts
across function boundaries is handled by the call-tree visitor
described in \S\ref{sec:assume-guarantee}.

\subsection{Worked example: a Transformer block}
\label{sec:ag:example}

Consider a single pre-norm Transformer block
$\mathit{Block} \;=\; \mathit{Add}\circ \mathit{MLP}\circ\mathit{LN}_2
\,\oplus_{+}\, \mathit{Add}\circ\mathit{Attn}\circ\mathit{LN}_1$.
With symbolic dimensions $(B,T,D)$ and head count $H\!\mid\!D$,
 synthesises six leaf contracts (two
\texttt{LayerNorm}, three projection \texttt{Linear}s inside attention,
one MLP) plus the join contract for the residual sum. Each leaf
discharges in $<\!50$\,ms; the four \textsc{Seq} entailments and the
two \textsc{Par} entailments (residual~+~QKV split) discharge in a
further $<\!100$\,ms. A \emph{non-modular} analysis would have to
inline the entire forward graph and pose a single SMT query whose size
is quadratic in $T$ once the attention mask is unfolded; in practice
this query times out for $T\!>\!512$. The compositional reading
keeps every per-leaf query under the decidable
fragment of \citet{cousot1977abstract}'s product domain and lets the
inter-leaf entailments inherit the abstraction, recovering a
\textsc{Verified} verdict for the whole block.
